## Why this archive exists

In 8 years our club went from **~50 cars** to **fewer than 15 in good condition**. Parts are getting scarce, mechanics who know the G13B engine are retiring, and the knowledge is being lost.

This site gathers everything we've learned over years keeping these little Japanese monsters alive: **factory manuals, repair tutorials, proven modifications, parts sources by country, and the worldwide community** that preserves them.

If you own a Swift GTi, a JDM Cultus, a Forsa, a Canadian/USA Swift GT, or a Geo Metro with conversion — **this site is yours**.

## The engine that makes it special

The **G13B DOHC 16V** is the reason these cars keep running 35 years later. Forged block, rods and pistons from the factory, pentroof cylinder head inspired by the Ford RS1600, automatic hydraulic lash adjusters. Handles up to 9,000 rpm on stock internals and produces up to **376 documented whp** with a properly built turbo.
